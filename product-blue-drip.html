THE STORY OF BLUE DRIP

Inspired by the untamed power and crisp freshness of the open ocean, Blue Drip is an olfactory masterpiece designed for those who command respect. It's a fresh aquatic escape contrasted by a seductive, warm sweetness that lingers long after you leave the room.

The scent opens with an instant burst of cooling sea salt, lavender, and crushed mint leaves, representing clean energy and raw confidence. As it drys, it transitions into an aromatic bouquet of rich sandalwood and jasmine, before resolving into a deep, magnetic base of cedar, amber, and tobacco. Designed as an Extrait de Parfum (using 30% Pure Perfume Oil imported from France), it ensures rich projectivity and unprecedented longevity.

HOW TO WEAR FOR 12+ HOUR WEAR

01

Focus on Pulse Points

Spray on warm pulse points—behind ears, base of throat, and wrists. Heat activates the Extrait oils.

02

Do Not Rub

Avoid rubbing wrists together after spraying. Rubbing breaks down delicate top note molecules, lessening duration.

03

Moisturize First

Apply fragrance to well-hydrated skin. Dry skin absorbs the perfume oils, making the scent fade quicker.

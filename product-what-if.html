THE STORY OF WHAT IF

Designed for the modern gentleman who embraces mystery and commands presence, What If is a powerhouse woody statement. It is a deep, sophisticated exploration of rich woods and warm resins, crafting a magnetic aura of structured confidence.

The fragrance opens with an elegant, spicy-woody spark of creamy Australian sandalwood, exotic cardamom, and black pepper. As it evolves, a dry, masculine heart of robust cedarwood, smoky vetiver, and papyrus unfolds. Finally, it settles into a rich, seductive foundation of dark amber, smooth leather, and a hint of vanilla. Formulated as a premium Extrait de Parfum with 20% concentration of imported French oils, it provides unparalleled longevity and projection that lasts all day and night.

HOW TO WEAR FOR 12+ HOUR WEAR

01

Focus on Pulse Points

Spray on warm pulse points—behind ears, base of throat, and wrists. Heat activates the Extrait oils.

02

Do Not Rub

Avoid rubbing wrists together after spraying. Rubbing breaks down delicate top note molecules, lessening duration.

03

Moisturize First

Apply fragrance to well-hydrated skin. Dry skin absorbs the perfume oils, making the scent fade quicker.
